Hyperbolica

Interactive Curved-Space Rendering

Breaking the Rules of Euclidean Geometry in C++17

Motivation & Goal

The Problem

Our screens and standard rendering engines (OpenGL) are fundamentally built for Euclidean (flat) geometry. Rendering spaces with constant negative curvature natively is impossible in 3D without distortion.

The Goal

To create a bespoke real-time engine that perfectly simulates walking through a hyperbolic plane, specifically visualising a {4,6} tiling (where 6 squares meet at every single vertex).

The Architecture

A strict separation of concerns allows us to do complex non-Euclidean math without confusing the graphics pipeline.

Math Layer
Hyperboloid model, metrics, distance calculations, isometries.

The Tessellation Challenge

In OpenGL, a line between two vertices is always drawn straight. But in hyperbolic space, straight lines (geodesics) appear beautifully curved when projected to our screen.

Dynamic Mesh Rebuilding

Edge Segments

If we try to draw a hyperbolic square with only 4 vertices, it collapses into flat geometry. By defining Edge Segments, we calculate intermediate vertices along the true geodesic path using math::geodesic_lerp.

Radial Bands

Just curving the edges isn't enough; the interior face of the tile must also correctly curve into the distance! Radial Bands tessellate the inner face from the center to the edges, ensuring perfect depth interpolation.

Infinite Walking

The Frame Re-basing Solution

  • The Issue: As you travel further from the origin (0,0) in hyperbolic space, coordinates grow exponentially. Floating point precision completely fails, causing jitter and tearing.
  • The Fix: We don't move the camera infinitely. We lock the camera within a "local tile".
  • Seamless Crossing: Every time we cross a tile edge, we secretly teleport back to the center of the new tile, applying a mathematical transformation (rebasing the frame) to make the transition invisible.

Interactive Debugging

Using Dear ImGui, we inject real-time controls over our engine's state:

  • Toggle Floor Grid & Wireframes to expose the underlying `{4,6}` tessellation.
  • Dynamically adjust Edge Segments & Radial Bands to see the mesh curvature rebuild instantly.
  • Control custom Lorentz-invariant distance fog algorithms directly in the shader.
